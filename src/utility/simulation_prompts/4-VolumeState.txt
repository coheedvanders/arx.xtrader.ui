# 1. PURPOSE

The purpose of `volumeState.ts` is to answer:

> **"How active is the market, how is volume changing, and does volume confirm or conflict with what price is doing?"**

This module should describe the volume environment around the current candle.

It should identify:

* relative volume
* volume expansion
* volume contraction
* volume regime
* volume transitions
* price-volume relationship
* directional volume interpretation
* volume strength
* explainable reasons

This is **not a trading signal**.

It is not supposed to independently predict LONG or SHORT.

Volume should primarily tell us:

> **How much participation is occurring and whether that participation confirms or conflicts with the current price behavior.**

# 3. CORE PRINCIPLE

Volume itself is not inherently bullish or bearish.

For example:

```text
High volume
```

does NOT automatically mean:

```text
BULLISH
```

and:

```text
Low volume
```

does NOT automatically mean:

```text
BEARISH
```

The interpretation depends on price behavior.

Examples:

```text
Price ↑ + Volume ↑
```

may indicate strong participation supporting the move.

```text
Price ↑ + Volume ↓
```

may indicate weak participation / lack of confirmation.

```text
Price ↓ + Volume ↑
```

may indicate aggressive selling participation.

```text
Price ↓ + Volume ↓
```

may indicate weak selling / declining participation.

The module must therefore distinguish:

### Volume state

from:

### Price-volume interpretation

# 6. VOLUME BASELINE

Calculate a causal baseline using previous candles.

For example:

```text
baseline = average volume over previous N candles
```

Important:

The baseline must NOT include future candles.

Prefer a configurable rolling window.

For example:

```ts
VOLUME_BASELINE_PERIOD
```

Do not blindly use a fixed 20-candle value if the existing architecture already has a standard period.

The baseline should be robust against individual volume spikes.

Consider whether a median or trimmed/robust baseline is more appropriate than a simple average.

If implementing a robust baseline, keep the reasoning clear and deterministic.

---

# 7. RELATIVE VOLUME

Calculate:

```text
relativeVolume = currentVolume / baselineVolume
```

Examples:

```text
0.50x → unusually low participation
1.00x → normal participation
1.50x → elevated participation
2.50x → strong volume expansion
```

Do not interpret relative volume as directional by itself.

It measures:

> **How unusual is current volume compared with the recent baseline?**

---

# 8. VOLUME EXPANSION

Detect when volume is meaningfully above its normal baseline.

For example:

```text
relativeVolume >= expansion threshold
```

But do not use an arbitrary threshold without putting it into configuration.


Possible state:

Do not make expansion directional.

Example:

```text

```

should still be:

```text

```

while directional interpretation belongs to price-volume relationship.

# 10. VOLUME TRANSITIONS

Transitions are more useful than static volume states.

Important transitions include:

```text
CONTRACTION → EXPANSION
NORMAL → EXPANSION
EXPANSION → CONTRACTION
CONTRACTION → NORMAL
NORMAL → CONTRACTION
```

The most important transition for price-action analysis is often:

```text
CONTRACTION → EXPANSION
```

because it can indicate that market participation is increasing after a period of inactivity.

Create something like:

```ts
export type VolumeTransitionType =
  | 'NONE'
  | 'CONTRACTION_TO_EXPANSION'
  | 'NORMAL_TO_EXPANSION'
  | 'EXPANSION_TO_CONTRACTION'
  | 'CONTRACTION_TO_NORMAL'
  | 'NORMAL_TO_CONTRACTION'
```

And:

```ts
export interface VolumeTransition {
  detected: boolean
  type: VolumeTransitionType
  strength: number
  reason: string[]
}
```

The transition must be determined causally using the current and previous volume states.

# 13. BULLISH VOLUME CONFIRMATION

A bullish price-volume confirmation can occur when:

```text
Price moves meaningfully upward
+
Volume expands
```

Especially when:

* candle has strong bullish structure
* price displacement is bullish
* close is strong
* volume is significantly above baseline

Example:

```text
Price +1.4%
Range = 1.8 ATR
Volume = 2.4x baseline
Close near high
```

This is stronger evidence of participation behind the move.

Reasons should include actual values:

```text
"Price increased 1.4%"
"Volume expanded to 2.4x baseline"
"Bullish candle closed near its high"
```

---

# 14. BEARISH VOLUME CONFIRMATION

Mirror the bullish case.

Example:

```text

Volume = 2.6x baseline
Close near low
```

This can indicate meaningful selling participation.

Example reasons:


# 15. BULLISH WEAKNESS

Consider a situation like:

```text
Price continues upward
+
volume declines
```

This does not automatically mean reversal.

Instead, describe it as:

> **Bullish price movement with declining participation.**

This may indicate:

* weakening participation
* lack of confirmation
* potential exhaustion if extreme/contextually appropriate

Do NOT turn this automatically into a SHORT signal.

---

# 16. BEARISH WEAKNESS

```text
Price continues downward
+
volume declines
```

This may indicate:

> Bearish movement with declining participation.

Again:

Do not automatically classify this as bullish reversal.

It is a lack of confirmation, not proof of reversal.

# 17. EXHAUSTION

Volume spikes near an extreme price movement can sometimes indicate exhaustion.

However:

> High volume alone is NOT exhaustion.

Consider exhaustion only when multiple pieces of evidence align, such as:

```text
extreme relative volume
+
large directional movement
+
poor follow-through / poor close
+
large opposing wick
+
movement into a meaningful level
```

If sufficient context exists, classify it as:

```text
EXHAUSTION
```

Otherwise do not manufacture it.

Keep exhaustion conservative.

---

# 18. ABSORPTION

Do not automatically classify high volume as absorption.

Absorption requires evidence that:

```text
high participation
+
price fails to continue proportionally
+
price repeatedly interacts with an area
```

If the current architecture does not yet provide enough information to confidently detect absorption, do NOT invent it.

It is acceptable to return:

```text
NEUTRAL
```

instead.

Later modules such as Heatmap/liquidity analysis can provide stronger absorption evidence.
